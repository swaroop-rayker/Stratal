Layout decisions to review

  1. Five bands, most urgent first, as in SPEC MON-4: who and what state; what it is doing now and how far; the stage timeline; resources; the activity log. The log takes whatever height is left and scrolls on its own.
  2. The activity line is the largest text on the screen (14 px mono, bold), because "what is it doing right now" is the question this screen answers (MON-1.2). Progress sits right under it, with percent, step, rate and ETA in fixed-width columns so nothing moves as they change.
  3. Timeline bars are sized by duration, with a minimum width so short stages stay readable; the running stage grows as it runs and pending ones are dashed. A bar is a button: it opens a drawer with that stage's events, resource charts and peaks (MON-4.2), and the main charts and log then filter to that stage too.
  4. Five resource tiles with their own small charts (the last 5 minutes), in the fixed resource order. VRAM shows the driver's figure big and PyTorch's allocated and reserved below it, as a dashed line on the same chart (MON-2.4). GPU utilisation carries the tooltip SPEC asks for.
  5. The activity log has a This job / All jobs switch (approved in SCREENS.md, open point 2), level and stage filters, and pauses its auto-scroll as soon as you scroll up, with a "Jump to latest" button.
  6. One primary action per state: Cancel while queued or running (danger style, with a confirm); Retry when failed; Open output version when completed.
