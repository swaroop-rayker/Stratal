SCR-01 — App shell · hi-fi mockup v1

Gate 2, step 2 of 2

Built on the approved low-fi wireframe (SCR-01-lofi_v1.html, approved 2026-11-15), using only the design system v1 tokens and components (system-preview.html): every colour, size, radius and duration on this page is a token, copied unchanged. The data is realistic, taken from the M2 demo and the spec's examples. The buttons at the top switch the whole page between dark and light, and hide the numbered annotations. Frame A is also shown in light below it, so both themes can be seen together.

What changed since the low-fi, for review
  • Nothing in the layout. The three bands, positions and sizes are as approved (sidebar 216 px, top bar 48 px, status bar 28 px).
  • Seven icons are new (datasets, jobs, health, theme, shortcuts, chevron-down, retry). They are drawn on the design system's 16-px grid with its stroke, and would join its set.
  • The selected navigation item uses the table's "selected row" treatment (accent-soft fill and a 2-px accent edge), so selection looks the same everywhere.
  • Each resource in the status bar carries its fixed resource colour as an 8-px square and a sparkline; the numbers stay in text colour so they remain readable at 11 px.

Accessibility notes for implementation

  • Landmarks: nav (main navigation), header, main, footer (machine resources). The resource strip is one aria-live="off" region: numbers change every second and must not be read out; its full values are in its accessible name.
  • The job indicator is a button whose name states the job, state, stage and percent. State changes are announced once through role="status"; failures through role="alert".
  • Every state carries an icon and a word; resource colours are always beside their label.
  • Tab order: navigation, location, job indicator, content, status bar.
