Layout decisions to review

  1. Top to bottom: identity, where it came from, what it holds, then the rows. The header names the version; a lineage strip shows its chain back to raw and anything made from it; four status tiles summarise its rows; tabs below hold the detail.
  2. The primary button is the next step in the flow, chosen from the version's state: Continue the pipeline while stages remain; Start a review once it has captions (phase 1, after M4); Build the eval report once a review is closed. The other actions are secondary buttons beside it, disabled with a reason when not yet possible.
  3. The status tiles are also filters. Clicking "duplicate 340" opens the Rows tab filtered to duplicates. One click from "how many" to "which ones".
  4. Three tabs: Rows (the manifest, virtualised, with a thumbnail and the stage's own columns), Reasons (non-accepted rows by reason and by the stage that decided them) and Config (the resolved stage config and the identity hashes).
  5. A row opens a drawer with the image and every manifest field. For a duplicate, the drawer shows the whole group side by side with the kept one marked, the kind and the pHash distance. This is where DATA-2.1 and DATA-2.2 decisions can be checked by eye.
  6. The lineage strip is linear with forks. Ancestors run left to right to this version; descendants fork to the right. Clicking a node opens that version. A full graph view is not needed in phase 1.
