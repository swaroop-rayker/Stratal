Layout and hierarchy only, inside the approved SCR-01 shell (faint). Blue numbers are annotations. A training job uses the same page as a pipeline job (approved SCR-04 v1): the same header, activity line, resource tiles and activity log. What changes is the middle: training charts instead of a stage-only view, and a timeline of epochs instead of stages. The data: job_0031, a small CNN on the CIFAR-10 split, epoch 7 of 20.

Layout decisions to review

  1. Same page, same five bands as v1, so a person reads a training run exactly as a pipeline run: who and what state; what it is doing now and how far; the timeline; then (new) the training charts; the resources; the log.
  2. Progress counts epochs and steps. The bar is the whole run; the line under it says "epoch 7 / 20 · step 140 / 312", samples per second and ETA, in fixed-width columns as in v1.
  3. Five small training charts in one row, loss and accuracy wider than the rest: loss (train per step as a line, validation per epoch as dots), accuracy (train and validation), learning rate, gradient norm, step time. Each has its latest value in large mono and a table view (the accessibility rule).
  4. The timeline shows epochs as equal segments. A diamond marks each checkpoint; the filled one is the one kept (only the latest is: REG-2.6). A pause appears as a dashed gap with its reason.
  5. Device and why sit in the header line, because "why is this on the CPU?" is the first question about a slow run (RUN-1.9).
  6. One banner per unusual state, above the activity line: paused (by the person, or by heat with the temperatures), stalled (no progress for 5 minutes), each with its one action.
  7. Actions by state: running: Pause and Cancel; paused: Resume and Cancel; failed with out-of-memory: Start again with 128 (opens SCR-09 prefilled); completed: Open the model (SCR-11).
