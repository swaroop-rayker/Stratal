Serves DATA-7.5 (any tabular version as a table of columns) and shows DATA-7.3's encodings. Built on the approved low-fi wireframe (SCR-03columns-lofi_v1.html, approved 2026-11-24 with its question: distinct counts exact up to 10,000) inside SCR-03's approved look: header, lineage nodes, status tiles, tabs and dense table, unchanged. Frame A is wine's raw version ds_b3f4ff09de8a; frame B the version Flow 6's transform would make (its figures are wine's after the four operations); frame C power.parquet as it would read once ingested with Global_active_power as the target (not ingested yet; the figures are computed from the file). Every number is computed from the source files. Open as SCR-03columns_v1.html#light to start in light.

Decisions made in this step, for review
  • Bars in categorical 1, as the Distributions tab's here bars, on a baseline in border-strong; every sparkline has its own scale and an aria-label with its numbers, and the min · median · max beside it is the readable form, the median in text-primary.
  • The target is a target badge in the Role column, in the badge's grey: role is a word, not a colour.
  • Encoding in mono, as in the schema; "—" in muted for a number used as a number or for the target; a default shows as one_hot with a muted "default" after it; ignore dims the whole row to text-muted.
  • Zeros in the Missing column are muted, so a non-zero count stands out without a colour of its own; a non-zero count reads "1,204 · 0.6 %" in text-primary.
  • The expanded row takes the selected-row look (accent-soft, accent edge) and opens a sunken panel: a 20-bin histogram with the median as a dashed line labelled in mono, the axis under it, a note, and the bins as two dense tables beside it.
  • A transformed version's operations sit in an info callout above the table, each operation in mono, with Edit the transform… at its right (frame B).
  • Rows not counted are one secondary phrase at the end of the summary, the count in mono, with Show them as a link in accent (frame C).

Accessibility notes

  • Each column row is a button row: Enter expands it (aria-expanded), j / k move between rows, headers sort with Enter and carry aria-sort.
  • Every sparkline and histogram is an image with its numbers in its label, and the expanded panel lists every bin as a table; the min · median · max is text.
  • Role, encoding and status are words; muted zeros and dimmed ignored rows are also said in words (the "0" and the "ignore").
  • Contrast uses the text tokens on --surface, --surface-raised and --surface-sunken; measured in both themes before this was sent.
