Layout decisions to review

  1. One page, three tabs. Overview covers a time range and holds the resource history, alerts, diagnostics, and loads and unloads. Compare two jobs covers MON-4.4. History covers MON-4.5, P2; the tab appears only if it is built. The range control and the monitor's health sit in the header, above the tabs.
  2. The range: presets (1 h, 6 h, 24 h, 7 d) and Custom…, with 1 h as the default (question 1). A range that ends now extends live. Beyond 7 days the lanes draw one-minute averages (NFR-12), and the lane's label says so.
  3. Resource history is five stacked lanes on one time axis: CPU, RAM, GPU, VRAM and temperature, not one chart with five axes. Each threshold is a labelled dashed line: RAM 85 %, VRAM 90 %, and the pause and resume temperatures. A jobs strip above the lanes shows each job in the range as a band split into its stages. On the strip, ▲ marks an alert and ◆ a model load or unload. Clicking a band narrows the range to that job. One crosshair reads all five lanes. GPU keeps NVML's tooltip (MON-2's note).
  4. Alerts are a table, one row per alert: when, what and its value against the threshold, the job and stage (or "no job"), the action it caused, and how long it lasted. A row opens a drawer with the alert, each action it caused (through caused_by), its clearing, and a link to the job's log on SCR-04 (frame B).
  5. Diagnostics have two parts. Now is one line per running stage: its expected resource (MON-3's table) and three observed figures over the last 30 s, each a small bar with its threshold. The figures are GPU %, the job's CPU in cores against what it can use, and disk reads. When a rule fires, the sentence and its suggested action follow. Earlier lists past diagnoses, with how long each lasted.
  6. Loads and unloads are a table with PyTorch's allocated memory and the card's figure side by side (K6): before → after for each, plus the time taken. PyTorch's reserved memory is in the row's tooltip, so its gap from allocated shows the caching allocator (MON-2.4).
  7. Compare two jobs:
    • Two pickers: training runs first, then any job.
    • A segmented By time running | By epoch control. By epoch is disabled, with its reason, unless both jobs are training runs.
    • Small multiples of GPU, card VRAM, the job's RSS and temperature, then a table of means and peaks.
    • A is a solid line and B a dashed one, so the two are told apart by more than colour.
    • The machine lanes say that they include anything else that was running.
  8. Live: new alerts, diagnoses and loads come in through the existing global stream and appear at the top of their table without moving the rows below. Each table holds the 50 most recent in the range, with Show more.

Two questions this layout depends on

  1. The default range: the last hour, or the last job's span? I propose 1 h, which answers "what is the machine doing", plus a click on a job band to zoom to it. The alternative opens on the last job, which can be days old.
  2. Should SCR-04 get Compare with… in its header? It would open this tab with that job as A. K11 limits SCR-04's addendum to the alert and diagnostic lines, so this widens it slightly. I propose yes. Otherwise both jobs are picked here.
