Decisions made in this step, for review
  • The type is chosen with the Train a model drawer's radio cards (SCR-09 v2, approved): accent-soft with an accent edge when chosen. Once chosen, the four cards fold into one line with Change, as that drawer folds its step 1, so the stages get the room. From a version it is one dashed card, as a kind that cannot be chosen there.
  • The stop is a native radio in accent labelled "Stop after" on each row; the chosen row takes the accent edge. Stages after it sit on --surface-sunken with "not run · …" in their line: a fill and words, not hatching, so the text keeps its contrast.
  • The transform switch is the design system's toggle: it acts at once (it opens SCR-15), which is what a toggle is for; the stop radios are submitted with the form, as SCR-02's checkboxes were. From a folder the toggle is replaced by a Stop after ingest button, so nothing disabled sits in the tab order without a use.
  • Stage rows have two lines: the name in mono with its chevron, and what it does with its key setting in secondary; 44 px high, against 36 px for the phase-1 rows.
  • Start job names the stop ("Start job · to split"), so the button says what the radios chose.
  • Notices (type changed, nothing left to run) are info callouts, with Undo on a type change.

Accessibility notes

  • The type cards are one fieldset of native radios with a legend; the stops are one radio group labelled "Stop after", one radio per stage, so the arrow keys move the stop and a screen reader reads "Stop after, split, 4 of 4".
  • A not-run stage says "not run" in its text; the sunken fill is not the only cue. The chosen stop also names itself in Start job.
  • The transform toggle has its effect as its description ("Opens SCR-15 for ds_…"), so switching it is never a surprise.
  • Contrast is measured in both themes before this was sent; muted text on the sunken fill and on the chosen card uses the approved tokens.
