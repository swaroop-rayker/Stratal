Layout and hierarchy only. Greys are deliberate: colour, type, icons and component styling come in the hi-fi mockup and use only the approved design system. Data is realistic so sizes are honest. Blue numbers are annotations, not UI.

Layout decisions to review

  1. Three bands. Sidebar for navigation; a top bar for where am I and is a job running; a thin status bar at the bottom for machine vitals and connection. The active job is at the top because it is what you act on; the resources are at the bottom because they are glanced at, like an editor's status bar.
  2. Active-job indicator sits at the right of the top bar, in the same place on every screen (design principle 3). One job: its pill, stage, progress and percent. Several: a count that opens a short list.
  3. Resource strip shows the five fixed resources with a 40-px sparkline each, plus the stream state. It never changes layout as numbers change (principle 4).
  4. Health is a dot and a word in the sidebar footer; a failed check turns into a full-width banner under the top bar.
  5. Toasts stack bottom right, above the status bar. Failures stay until dismissed and carry the suggested action.
  6. At 1024 px the sidebar becomes a 56-px icon rail (labels in tooltips and in the shortcut help), and the strip drops sparklines but keeps every value.
  7. Hidden until built: Models, Chat, System and Settings do not appear in phase 1. The dashed box only marks where they will go.
