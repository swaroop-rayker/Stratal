SCR-09 v2 — Training entry and fine-tuning · hi-fi mockup v1

Gate 2, step 2 of 2

One mockup for the SCR-09 v2 gate (K11), built on the two approved low-fi wireframes: the training entry (SCR-09v2-entry-lofi_v1.html) and fine-tuning (SCR-09v2-lofi_v1.html), both approved 2026-11-20. It uses only design system v1 tokens, components and patterns, plus the contrast rules approved at earlier Gate 3s, with no new icon. What SCR-09 already has keeps its approved look (SCR-09_v1, SCR-09hpo_v1). All data was read from the running stack on 2026-11-20, except frame F's cifar10-vehicles, which is illustrative, as in the low-fi. Open as SCR-09v2_v1.html#light to start in light.

Decisions made in this step, for review
  • The drawer is the built app's drawer (440 px, from the Start pipeline drawer; the low-fi said 480 px, the built one is 440 px), with a step bar under its header: two numbered steps, the current one in accent, a finished one with a check.
  • Kinds are selectable cards with a native radio in accent, in one fieldset. The chosen card takes the selected-row look (accent-soft, accent inset edge). A kind the split does not allow is a dashed card with no fill, its title in text-secondary and its reason beside the info icon. The design system had no radio card; this composes the card, the radio's accent and the disabled tile of SCR-08's palette.
  • Step 1's facts are a small surface box, label and value in two columns, values in mono where they are figures.
  • The chosen kind on SCR-09 is a neutral tag (the badge shape at 24 px, with the kind's icon) followed by a ghost Change…, where v1 had the segmented control.
  • The "spec left behind" warning is the warning callout inside the drawer, under the kinds, and the primary button names the new kind (Use Classical) with Keep the network beside it.
  • Fine-tuning's Start from card is a segmented control (Fresh weights · A trained version) like the Device card, a version select, then the compatibility lines with a success check or danger cross and both values, the Reset the head toggle from the design system, and an info callout naming where the version registers.
  • The freeze list is a dense table with checkboxes; frozen rows on surface-sunken, and Freeze up to here as a ghost button on the hovered row. The total under it is in mono.
  • Refusals use the danger callout on the Start from card (as SCR-09's out-of-memory panel); a classical model's "always start fresh" is an info callout, not an error.
