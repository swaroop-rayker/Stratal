SCR-13 — System monitor · hi-fi mockup v1

Gate 2, step 2 of 2

Built on the approved low-fi wireframe (SCR-13-lofi_v1.html, approved 2026-11-22 with both of its questions: the last hour by default, and Compare with… on SCR-04). It uses SCR-14's phase-2 shell and only design system v1 tokens and components. Resource colours are fixed app-wide. The data is real unless marked. Frame A's lanes are the monitor's samples around coco10k's first pipeline, job_0026 on 17 Nov, in 20-second means, and its loads and unloads are that job's own events. No alert has fired on this laptop yet, so the alert and diagnostic rows are illustrative, and they are marked. Frame C overlays two real Small CNN runs on CIFAR-10's split. Times are local. Lanes are static SVG here; the real screen uses ECharts, as SCR-04 does.

Decisions made in this step, for review
  • System joins the side navigation's foot above Settings, with the chart icon and g s. The foot's health line stays, so the app is never more than one click from the monitor.
  • The header's range is a segmented group of 1 h · 6 h · 24 h · 7 d and Custom…; a custom range shows its own dates. The monitor's health is a pill beside it: a success heart with "Monitor reporting · 1 s ago". It turns into the error state of frame D when the monitor stops.
  • Lanes draw a 1.5 px line in each resource's fixed colour, on a hairline baseline. Thresholds are dashed lines in text-muted, with their value in mono at the right end. One crosshair in text-secondary reads all five lanes through a raised tooltip, each value beside its resource square.
  • The jobs strip sits on surface-sunken. Each stage is a band in its final state's colour (16 % fill, 45 % border), with the job ID on its longest band. Loads and unloads are small text-secondary diamonds, and alerts are the warning icon. Each marker has a tooltip.
  • Status pills follow the design system's existing contrast rules: in light, mixed 80 % toward text-primary; in dark, 70 % on a selected or hovered row (SCR-04 G3, SCR-07 G3). Without them, the paused pill on the selected alert measured 4.28:1.
  • Alerts use the dense table. The alert shows the warning icon and its name, its value against the threshold is in mono, and the action is the state pill it caused (paused, stalled, failed) or "warned only" in secondary text. The row whose drawer is open takes the selected-row look.
  • Diagnostics' Now gives each running stage a raised box with the expected resource as a neutral badge. It has three bars: GPU in the GPU colour, the job's CPU in the CPU colour, and disk reads in text-secondary. Each threshold is a 2 px text-primary tick. A diagnosis is a warning callout with the alert icon; "as expected" is a line with a success check.
  • Loads and unloads put the event first, with a down or up chevron, then PyTorch allocated before the card's figure (K6), each as "a → b GB" in mono.
  • Compare draws A solid and B dashed in each lane's resource colour; the legend shows the two dashes with the job IDs. Pickers are select buttons that show the job, model and length. The summary table marks no "better" value, since resource figures are not scores.
  • The alert's drawer is 470 px. Its header has the warning badge and a cleared or firing pill. What it caused is a vertical timeline using the log's icons: pause, the clearing, resume.

Accessibility notes for implementation

  • Each lane is an image with a text label naming its resource, unit and range maximum; the Table view lists the means. Thresholds are named in the lane's label, not only drawn.
  • Alerts and diagnoses enter an aria-live="polite" region as one sentence each ("GPU hot, 84 °C: paused job_0026"); figures in the lanes are not announced.
  • A and B differ by dash as well as by the legend; markers on the jobs strip are focusable, with their tooltip as the accessible name.
