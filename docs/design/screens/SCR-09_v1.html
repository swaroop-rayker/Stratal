SCR-09 — Training run setup · hi-fi mockup v1

Gate 2, step 2 of 2

Built on the approved low-fi wireframe (SCR-09-lofi_v1.html, approved 2026-11-19) inside the SCR-01 shell, using only design system v1 tokens, components and patterns; no new icon. The data: the built-in Small CNN (288,746 parameters) on the CIFAR-10 split ds_f8e67211dd94. Memory figures are the builder's estimate (ADR-0017 rule 7); on the card the same CNN measured 0.23 GB at batch 128 and 0.79 GB at 512 (M8). The study part (M10) is drawn collapsed only. Open as SCR-09_v1.html#light to start in light.

Decisions made in this step, for review
  • Sections are cards with a heading and the requirements they serve, as on SCR-14; the training form uses the same schema-form pattern, three fields to a row, grouped under small headings (schedule, optimizer, reproducibility, checkpoints).
  • The VRAM bar uses the VRAM resource colour, as the status bar does: solid for weights, gradients and optimizer state; lighter for activations; muted for the CUDA context; a white tick for the 5.1 GB cap. Every part also has its number in the table under it.
  • The result is a callout: success "Fits under the cap", or warning "Over the cap by the estimate" with Use batch 1024 as the primary action and Start anyway beside it. Warning, not danger: nothing is blocked.
  • A prefilled field (from "Start again with 64") gets the info badge changed and "was 128 in job_0067" as its helper, like a changed setting on SCR-14.
  • Start run is the page's only primary button, at the bottom of the right-hand column, with what will happen just above it.

Accessibility notes

  • Fields are labelled by their property names; the helper, or the error that replaces it, is their description; an invalid field has aria-invalid and focus moves to the first one after a refused start.
  • The VRAM result is a polite status; the bar itself is hidden from screen readers, since the table beside it says the same in words.
  • Ctrl+Enter starts the run from anywhere on the page.
  • Over the cap is a warning with an icon and words, never only the bar's length or colour.
