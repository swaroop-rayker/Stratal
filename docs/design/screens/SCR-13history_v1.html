SCR-13 — System monitor, History tab (MON-4.5) · hi-fi mockup v1

Gate 2, step 2 of 2

Built on the approved low-fi wireframe (SCR-13history-lofi_v1.html, approved 2026-11-22 with its three recommendations: one stage at a time, the latest run's group by default, the last 20 runs). It reuses SCR-13's approved hi-fi as built: its shell, tabs, monitor pill, lanes, tooltip, tables and states, and only design system v1 tokens. The data is real, from the dev database: frame A is every run of train on the GPU in one group, frame B every run of label, frame C the last 20 GPU training runs. Times are local. Lanes are static here; the real screen draws them with ECharts, as the Overview does.

Decisions made in this step, for review
  • History is the third tab, after Overview and Compare two jobs, at /system?tab=history. The header's range group is not shown on this tab, which counts runs, not hours; the monitor pill stays.
  • Controls sit in one row under the tabs: Stage and Group are select buttons in mono that show the name and the count (train · GPU · 30 runs), Last is a segmented group of 10, 20, 50 and 100, and Include stages reused from the cache is a checkbox with its count, disabled with "Training never reuses a run" for training.
  • Lanes are points, one per run, 9 px in the lane's colour, joined by a 1 px line at 70 % so the eye follows the order. The figures that are resources keep their fixed colours (VRAM and PyTorch in VRAM's, RAM in RAM's, temperature in temperature's). Rate and time are not resources, so they take categorical 1, as SCR-10's and SCR-11's figures do.
  • The group's median is a dashed line in text-secondary with its value in mono at the right end; thresholds are dashed in text-muted with their value, exactly as on the Overview. The latest run has a text-primary ring.
  • Tags on unusual runs are short mono notes in text-secondary on the surface, beside their point ("11.0 · worker lost, resumed"); the table's Note says the same. A run with no rate is a hollow point at the foot of its lane, tagged reused.
  • The tooltip is the Overview's raised tooltip: the run, when, its group, the lane's figure and, for training, its attempts. Hovering a point highlights its row in the table and the reverse.
  • The runs table is the dense table with a checkbox column. Compare these two is enabled with exactly two ticked and opens Compare two jobs with the older run as A. A row opens SCR-04.
  • All groups gives each group a shape and a categorical colour (circle, hollow circle, square, diamond, triangle in categorical 1 to 5), with a key above the lanes, so groups are told apart without colour.

Accessibility notes for implementation

  • Each lane is an image whose label names the figure, unit, median and the runs tagged; the table under the lanes is the full text equivalent, and Table only hides the lanes.
  • Points are focusable in run order (left and right arrows move between runs, across lanes the same run); focus shows the tooltip and highlights the row.
  • Hollow points, tags and shapes carry meaning beside colour; the latest run's ring is repeated as "latest" in the lane's label.
  • A run joining live is announced once in an aria-live="polite" region ("job_0119 joined: 6.4 s an epoch, median 6.4").
