Gate 2, step 2 of 2

Serves DATA-5.3 and LLM-2.1 (and the citation path from SCR-12) at /versions/:version_id and ?row=. It follows the approved low-fi wireframe (SCR-03corpora-lofi_v1.html, 2026-11-27), SCREENS.md v5 and ADR-0022 rules 1–4 and 13, inside the approved SCR-03 (v1, v2 and v4 mockups): its header, lineage nodes, tiles, tabs and row drawer are reused unchanged. Data as in the low-fi: the 36 real documents of Stratal's corpus and their sizes, the seven .json files under docs/spikes/ rejected, chunk counts estimated from the sections (about 970 at 256 tokens), placeholder version IDs (ds_…), and an instruction pair written for the mockup.

Decisions to review (beyond the approved low-fi)
  • The tiles on a corpus are facts, not filters: chunks, documents, vectors, the index's size and the model are not row statuses, so they use the tile's shape without its coloured edge or pressed state (the status tiles of images and tables keep theirs). On a documents version the usual status tiles return (accepted, rejected), since those do filter the rows.
  • Ask this index is the header's primary button (it is what an index is for); Build the retrieval report is secondary with the report icon; Continue the pipeline stays where it is.
  • The overlap in a chunk is the sunken surface with a dashed "overlap" tag before it, in text-secondary, so it reads as "repeated from the previous chunk" without a colour of its own.
  • "Cited as [1]" is an info callout at the top of the drawer, with the citation number drawn exactly as in SCR-12 (accent box), and Back to the conversation as a link.
  • The drawer's two new type cards are the approved drawer's radio cards; the corpus chain uses the approved stage rows with one Stop after radio, the stop row with the accent edge. The embedding model is plain mono text with "fixed" (one model, decision 4): not a disabled input that looks editable.
  • The chat format of an instruction pair is a sunken mono block; the template's own tokens (<|im_start|>…) are in text-secondary, the pair's text in text-primary, so the content stands out from the format.

Accessibility notes

  • Keyboard path. The header's actions, the lineage nodes, the tabs (arrow keys), the filter and search (/), then the rows (j / k, Enter opens the drawer). In the drawer: Back to the conversation, Previous / Next ([ / ]), close with Esc, focus returning to the row.
  • Opened from a citation, the drawer has focus and its title is announced with "cited as 1".
  • Never colour alone: the overlap has a written tag; statuses read accepted / rejected with their reason; the chosen type and stop are native radios.
  • Contrast uses the approved text tokens on surface, raised and sunken; the citation number in light uses the 90 % mix approved with SCR-12. Checked by measurement before review.
