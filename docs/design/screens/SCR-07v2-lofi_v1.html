Only what v2 adds is new: pause and resume, paused and stalled jobs, training and retention jobs.

Layout decisions to review

  1. Pause on running rows, Resume on paused ones, beside Cancel, for pipelines and training alike (OC-8). Pause asks no confirmation, because it loses nothing and is undone by Resume; the pill reads "Pausing…" until the job reaches its next safe point.
  2. Paused and stalled jobs stay in Active, because they need a decision. "Now / outcome" says why: paused by you, or for heat with the temperatures; stalled with how long.
  3. A training row reads "model on dataset" in the Dataset column, its progress in epochs, and its outcome as the registered model version with its validation score.
  4. A training job that ran out of memory offers "Start again with 128" instead of Retry, as on SCR-04 v2: the same batch would fail the same way. A failed pipeline keeps Retry.
  5. Retention jobs (ADR-0015) are listed too; their outcome is what they freed. Kind gains train and retention.
  6. The state group keeps its five buttons. Active now counts queued, running, paused and stalled.
