Layout and hierarchy only, inside the approved shell (faint). Blue numbers are annotations. Two studies supply the data. The first tunes the Small CNN's learning rate, batch size and weight decay on CIFAR-10 (ds_f8e67211dd94), 10 epochs per trial; M9's demo trained that model in 65 s for 10 epochs. The second tunes a random forest on breast cancer (569 rows; 455 / 57 / 57) with 5-fold cross-validation. The figures are made up but plausible; S-09 measures the real per-trial costs.

Layout decisions to review

  1. One page per study, top to bottom: a header with the study's state, its two limits and the objective; then the optimisation history and the best configuration side by side; then the trial table; and, when the study has closed, parameter importance and the test evaluation.
  2. Both limits are shown as progress: trials started of n_trials, and running time of the budget, which excludes time spent paused. Whichever limit is reached first ends the study, and the header says which one did (HPO-1.2).
  3. The history chart plots each trial's value as a dot, with a step line for the best so far. A pruned trial is a hollow dot at its last reported value, and a failed trial a cross on the axis. Every chart has a table view (DESIGN-SYSTEM accessibility).
  4. The trial table is sortable and virtualised. Its columns: number, state, value, each tuned parameter, the step reached, duration, and (for a deep-learning trial) its job. The state is always written in words: complete, pruned at epoch 3 · median, pruned at epoch 6 · budget, failed · invalid_combination, running (HPO-1.3). The best trial's row is marked with ★ and the word "best".
  5. A trial's row opens a drawer. It shows the trial's intermediate values per step against the median of earlier trials, which is the line the pruner compared it with, so a pruning decision can be checked. It also links to the trial's job on SCR-04 (deep learning only).
  6. Parameter importance appears once the study has closed (fANOVA, ADR-0018 rule 9), as bars with a table view. Before that, the section says it is computed at the end. With fewer than two complete trials, or only one tuned parameter, it says why there is none.
  7. The test evaluation is labelled "computed once". It shows the time it was computed, the registered version and its task metrics (EVAL-1.2). There is no button to run it again, anywhere (HPO-1.4).
  8. Actions: Open the best model (SCR-11, once registered), Start a run from the best configuration (SCR-09 prefilled), and, while the study runs, Pause, Resume and Cancel, which act on the current trial. Cancel asks first, because a cancelled study registers nothing.
  9. No list screen for studies. A study is reached from its job on SCR-07 (kind study), from a trial's SCR-04, and from a registered version on SCR-11 ("from study st_…"). On SCR-07 and SCR-04 that needs two small additions: a study kind with trials grouped under it, and "Trial 7 of st_…" in a trial's header. They are drawn at those screens' Gate 3 addenda, as M8's registry links were.
